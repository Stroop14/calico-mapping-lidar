A walk-through of a real 3-D laser scan of the Silver King Mine tunnel in the Calico Hills, captured with a handheld GeoSLAM scanner during the 2019 CSUSB geology field camp led by Dr. Erik Melchiorre. Field camp is the capstone of a geology education: days of nonstop fieldwork in the heat, and here, underground in clay dust. About 13.7 million measured points are streamed into your browser (~185 MB uncompressed on first visit), then you can walk the tunnel with a virtual headlamp. Colours are artistic, not true colour — the scanner recorded shape only.
