Reproduce the Silver King walkthrough. Do not invent a different pipeline.

Live reference: https://stroop14.github.io/calico-mapping-lidar/
LAS: https://github.com/Stroop14/calico-mapping-lidar/releases/tag/v1.0.0
Method: howto/RECIPE.md
Player (public domain, feet): player-controls/first-person.js

Units: **feet**. If the scanner exported metric, convert once (× 3.28084).

1. Open the LAS. Report point count, and whether RGB and intensity are actually filled. On Silver King both were zero — do not fake scanner color.
2. Two clouds: merge. Origin at the portal. Adit N17.5°E unless the new scan says otherwise.
3. Thin to about **½ inch**. Keep the floor. Cart-track grooves (ore-cart ruts, not railroad) must survive.
4. First-person page, not an orbit globe:
   - click to look, WASD
   - eye **5 ft 4 in** above the floor
   - one warm headlamp on the camera, aimed slightly down
5. Color from the attached wall photos: reddish-brown clay, cream/ochre mottling, dusty tan floor, timber brown if a portal is in the photos.
6. A simple page that opens the walk. Distances in feet.
7. Look at it against the photos. Fix clay, tracks, timber, light.

Do not add a sun or a metric HUD. Ship the walk.
