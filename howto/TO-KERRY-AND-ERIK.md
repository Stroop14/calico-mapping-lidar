That is the two GeoSLAM files from 24 March 2019, in a browser. You walk it.

After the LAS, the sequence is CloudCompare (about 50%), a free web converter called Potree so you can spin the cloud (about 75%), then a first-person walk (the last 25%). That last page is what I built. Color came from the wall photos; the scanner stored none.

Those first two programs were already free. Other people wrote them. I put the walk and the W-A-S-D controls on GitHub. The controls are public domain. My page is MIT.

https://github.com/Stroop14/calico-mapping-lidar

If you do not want to click through CloudCompare by hand: install Cursor, set the model to **Opus 5.5** (or the latest Grok), attach the LAS and two or three wall photos, and paste the prompt in howto/CURSOR-PROMPT.md. It runs the same tools, same order. You do not write code. You look at the clay and the cart tracks and say if they are wrong.
