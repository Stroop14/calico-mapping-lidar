Silver King Mine

Drop a scan. Walk it.

Drop a LAS, PLY, or XYZ file. You start inside the scan. Move fast with the mouse and W A S D, mark key points, end the walk, view it here, then download the site.

Color

Floor the ground
Wall the side of the tunnel
Roof the ceiling

The scan stays in this browser. This site does not keep a copy. Pick a color for the floor, the wall, and the roof, or drop a photo on that box. The color controls stay on the screen after the scan opens. Floor is the ground. Wall is the side of the tunnel. Roof is the ceiling, the rock above your head. One photograph in the big box is enough if you do not have three: the bottom of that picture colors the floor, the middle colors the wall, and the top colors the roof.

To practice on the scan that already worked, download the combined Silver King cloud, the 1.25 cm merge of the two walks: silver-king-combined.las.zip. Unzip it, then drop the LAS file on the box above.

Key points

You start inside the scan. W A S D moves fast, and Shift is faster. Drag with the left mouse button to turn your head, or click so the mouse looks for you. Drag with the right button to slide sideways and up. The mouse wheel moves you in and out. The same list stays on the screen.

Press K, or Key point, along the scan. Move a long way and press K again. The orange dot is the first key point. The green dot is the last one. At the end, press End the walk. Then press Play to view it here, and download the site.

If the scans do not already line up

Do that in CloudCompare before you drop the file. Line the scans up on the overlap, then merge them into one cloud. Delete only points that are nowhere near the tunnel. Keep the walls, a minecart, and a person the scanner caught. Save LAS.

The same program on your own computer

You do not install anything to use this page. The folder tools/mine-walk is that same program as a Python file, for a scan that is too big for the browser. It needs two free add-ons, and they are not part of the scan. NumPy holds the list of point coordinates. Pillow opens the photograph files so the colors can be read. This line downloads those two add-ons onto the computer that will run the program:

pip install numpy pillow

Then point it at the scan and the photographs. A short drift is thinned to about 2 cm. Silver King itself is 1.25 cm.

python3 tools/mine-walk/make_walk.py drift.las --photos wall.jpg --out site
cd site
python3 -m http.server 8000
