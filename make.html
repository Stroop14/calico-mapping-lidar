Silver King Mine

Drop a scan. Walk it.

Drop a LAS, PLY, or XYZ file. Add photographs if you have them. The walk opens on this page, with the same headlamp and fog as the Silver King mine. Then download the site and put it on your own host.

The scan stays in this browser. This site does not keep a copy.

If the scans do not already line up

Do that in CloudCompare before you drop the file. Line the scans up on the overlap, then merge them into one cloud. Delete only points that are nowhere near the tunnel. Keep the walls, a minecart, and a person the scanner caught. Save LAS.

The same steps on your own computer

The program is in this site’s repository, folder tools/mine-walk. A short drift is thinned to about 2 cm. Silver King itself is 1.25 cm.

pip install numpy pillow
python3 tools/mine-walk/make_walk.py drift.las --photos wall.jpg --out site
cd site
python3 -m http.server 8000

Open the Silver King walk
